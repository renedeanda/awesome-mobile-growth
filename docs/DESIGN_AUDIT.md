# Design audit

## Direction

An editorial field lab: cool paper, midnight ink, exact blue, and warm coral for authored context. Serif display type carries judgment; system sans carries work; monospace carries evidence and labels.

## Release checks

- Light and dark appearance at 320, 390, tablet, and desktop widths
- 200% zoom without horizontal loss of content
- Keyboard order, visible focus, and 44px interactive targets
- Reduced-motion preference
- Legible generated Open Graph image
- Physical iPhone Safari and VoiceOver pass

Do not call the design complete from desktop screenshots alone.
