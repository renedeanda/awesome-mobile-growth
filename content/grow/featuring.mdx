## Ask to be featured, early

Apple's editors pick what appears on the Today, Games and Apps tabs, and you can nominate your app for consideration in App Store Connect under **Featuring Nominations**. A nomination doesn't guarantee placement. It tells the team what's coming and when.

Nominate for one of three reasons:

| Nomination type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| App Launch | A new app going live |
| New Content | A season, a collection, a new mode, a timely event |
| App Enhancements | A meaningful update, such as a redesign or a new platform |

## How far ahead

Apple's two pages give different minimums, so plan for the longer one.

> "Featuring lead time varies — please give our team a minimum of two weeks notice. For wider featuring consideration, we recommend submitting a nomination up to three months in advance."
> Apple, Getting featured on the App Store

> "we recommend submitting plans as early as possible, with a minimum lead time of 3 weeks."
> Apple, Nominate your app for featuring

Submit at least three weeks before the date, and up to three months ahead for anything big.

## What the form asks for

- **A detailed description.** Say what's new, who it's for and why now. Apple asks you to state the purpose and priority of the nomination clearly.
- **A publication date** or a custom date range.
- **Related apps**, up to 10 in total.
- **Platforms, countries or regions, and localizations.**
- **In-app events** to attach. They must already be approved or published, and in-app events only exist on iPhone and iPad apps.
- **Supplemental materials**: up to 5 URLs, including TestFlight public links, so the team can try what you're describing.
- **Helpful details**, such as accessibility and inclusivity work.

If you nominate often, you can import nominations in bulk from a CSV.

<p>Rollbook 3.0 adds a light meter that uses the phone camera, so film shooters can meter, shoot and log a frame without a separate meter. It ships on 4 November in 12 languages, with VoiceOver support for the whole logging flow. A TestFlight link is attached.</p>

## Make the nomination easy to say yes to

- Pair it with an [in-app event](/grow/in-app-events/) when there's something time-bound: an approved event can be attached to the nomination.
- Have the store page ready: current screenshots, a clear subtitle and, if you can, the new [product page header and search result assets](/store-page/creative-assets/).
- Send a build the team can open. A public TestFlight link counts as supplemental material.
