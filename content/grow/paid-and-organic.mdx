## Share what you learn between ads and ASO

Paid campaigns and your store page reach some of the same people, so each can teach the other:

- **Search ad terms show demand.** Apple Ads search term reports show which words people typed before tapping your ad. Words that convert well are candidates for your keyword field and captions.
- **Ad creative tests are fast.** An ad test often gives a usable read within days. A message that wins in ads is worth testing on your store page.
- **The store page tests what ads can't:** how people react after they've chosen to look closer.

Share the insight, not the asset. An ad has to stop a scroll; a screenshot has to support a decision. The same idea usually needs a different layout in each.

## Match the page to the ad

If an ad promises one specific thing, send people to a page that delivers it:

- **App Store:** custom product pages can be used in Apple Ads, and ad networks can open them through StoreKit.
- **Google Play:** custom store listings can be targeted to a Google Ads campaign.

Then carry the promise into the app with a deep link, so the first screen matches the ad. See [custom pages](/store-page/custom-pages/) for the limits on each store.

## Don't confuse credit with cause

Attribution tells you which path got credit for an install. It doesn't tell you how many people would have installed anyway.

<Example title="Brand search ads">
<p>You bid on your own app name and the campaign reports 2,000 installs a month at a low cost. Most of those people were already searching for you and would likely have installed from the organic result. The true gain is the installs you'd have lost to a competitor's ad, which is usually much smaller.</p>
</Example>

To measure real impact, run holdouts: pause a campaign in one country or for a few weeks and watch total installs, not just attributed ones. Apple's AdAttributionKit and SKAdNetwork, and Google's privacy-preserving measurement, give limited, delayed data by design, so plan for modeled answers, not exact counts.

## Read organic numbers carefully during campaigns

Paid activity changes organic results. More installs can lift your ranking for some terms, and ads appear alongside organic results in App Store search. When a campaign runs:

- Note its dates and budget in your change log.
- Expect App Store Search numbers to include the effect of ads; Apple counts search-result ads as part of the App Store Search source.
- Judge keyword or screenshot changes in periods without big campaign changes.
