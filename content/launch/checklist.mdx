## How to use this checklist

Work top to bottom, starting three weeks before your launch date. Add the date below to see when each phase starts, or put the phases in your calendar. Items marked **Apple** or **Google Play** apply to one store; everything else applies to both. Tick items as you go; your progress is saved in this browser.

## Where the details live

Each item has a guide behind it:

- Records, review and release: [iPhone](/launch/iphone/), [iPad](/launch/ipad/), [Mac](/launch/mac/), [Google Play](/launch/google-play/).
- Fields and limits: [App name, subtitle and description](/store-page/metadata/), [keywords](/store-page/keywords/).
- Images: [screenshots](/store-page/screenshots/), [headers and search assets](/store-page/creative-assets/).
- Learning after launch: [testing your store page](/store-page/testing/), [App Store metrics](/learn/metrics/).

Store rules change. Confirm the current requirements in App Store Connect and Play Console before you submit; this checklist links to the official pages from each guide.
