## Know the two gates before you start

Two Google Play rules decide your launch date more than anything in your code. Check both on day one.

**New personal accounts must run a closed test.** If you created a personal developer account after 13 November 2023, Play Console keeps Production disabled until you have run a closed test with at least 12 testers who stayed opted in for the last 14 days in a row. Organization accounts are exempt. Recruit testers early; two weeks is the minimum, not the plan.

**Your app must target a recent Android version.** From 31 August 2026, new apps and updates must target Android 16 (API level 36). Wear OS and Android Automotive apps need API 35; Android TV and Android XR apps need API 34. You can request an extension to 1 November 2026. Targeting API 36 changes behavior: on screens 600 dp and wider, your app can no longer lock orientation or aspect ratio, so test tablets and foldables.

## Fill in Play Console in this order

1. **Create the app.** Choose the default language, app or game, free or paid. Paid-to-free is allowed later; free-to-paid is not.
2. **App content declarations.** Privacy policy URL, app access (give login steps if parts are gated), ads, content rating questionnaire, target audience, Data safety form, government app and financial features declarations where they apply.
3. **Store listing.** Name, short description, full description, icon, feature graphic, screenshots.
4. **Testing track.** Upload an Android App Bundle to internal testing first, then closed testing.
5. **Countries and pricing.** Pick countries deliberately; you can add more later.

The Data safety form must cover every SDK in the app, including analytics, crash reporting and ads. Get the list from your build file, not from memory.

## Write the listing for people and for search

Google Play has no hidden keyword field. Search reads your visible text, so write it the way your customers talk, and never repeat words to game it.

| Field | Limit | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App name | 30 characters | Your brand plus, if it fits, what the app is |
| Short description | 80 characters | One sentence a stranger understands |
| Full description | 4,000 characters | The details: features, who it's for, what it costs |

## Test with tracks, then roll out in stages

Use the tracks in order. **Internal testing** (up to 100 testers) is available within minutes and is right for your team. **Closed testing** is where new personal accounts meet the 12-tester rule. **Open testing** lets anyone join from your store listing.

For production, use a **staged rollout**: release to a small percentage, watch the Android vitals crash and ANR rates for a day or two, then raise it. You can halt a rollout if something breaks.

Those two rates affect how visible your app is. Google's bad behavior thresholds are a [user-perceived crash rate](/learn/metrics/user-perceived-crash-rate/) of 1.09% and an [ANR rate](/learn/metrics/user-perceived-anr-rate/) of 0.47% overall, or 8% on any single phone model, over the last 28 days. Above them, Play may reduce your app's visibility and show a warning on your store listing.

Before each release, check on a real device: fresh install, upgrade from the previous version, purchase and restore, deep links, notifications, and a small phone plus a tablet or foldable. Targeting API 36 also means Android ignores orientation and resizability limits on large screens; the [large screens guide](/launch/large-screens/) covers what changes.

Before you upload graphics, run the icon, feature graphic and screenshots through the [Play icon and graphics checker](/tools/play-asset-checker/).

## Read the first weeks

Google Play's store listing reports changed in mid-2026. They now count **unique users who tap Install, Open or Pre-register** and report a click-through rate, where they previously reported acquisitions and conversion rate. The older acquisition metrics are still in the Statistics tab. If you compare before and after July 2026, compare like with like.

Games can also re-engage players on the **You tab**, which Google describes as "a brand new, personalized destination on the Play Store". Promotional content, YouTube video listings and Play Points coupons are open to all game developers there.

After launch, follow installs into your product analytics: activation, day-7 retention, reviews and crashes by device. A spike in installs from a new country with poor retention usually means the listing wasn't localized, not that the market loves you.
