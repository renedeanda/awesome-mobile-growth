## Start with the decision

“Try a blue icon” is an action. “Clarify whether the product or the emotional outcome should lead the page” is a decision. Tests are valuable when their result changes what you do.

## Write the hypothesis first

Record the audience, proposed change, expected behavior, primary measure, guardrails, dates, and stopping rule before the experiment begins. Choose one primary question; bundled changes obscure the lesson.

## Use the platform’s current tools

Apple provides product page optimization and custom product pages. Google Play provides store listing experiments and custom store listings. Their eligibility, traffic behavior, and reporting can change; confirm the current console documentation when planning a live test.

## Protect against false confidence

Seasonality, paid traffic, featuring, product releases, price changes, and measurement delays can all shape a result. Keep a contemporaneous log. “Inconclusive” is a useful result when the alternative is invented certainty.

## Carry the learning forward

The experiment is not done until the result, context, decision, and next question are recorded. Reuse the insight across copy, creative, onboarding, and product only as far as the evidence supports.
