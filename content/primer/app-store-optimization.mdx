## What ASO includes

- **Keywords:** the words people search with, placed in the fields each store reads. [Keywords guide](/store-page/keywords/)
- **Metadata:** the name, subtitle or short description, and description. [Metadata guide](/store-page/metadata/)
- **Visuals:** icon, screenshots, previews, and Apple's new header and search assets. [Screenshots guide](/store-page/screenshots/)
- **Ratings and reviews:** they show next to your app and Apple says they influence search ranking. [Ratings guide](/grow/ratings-reviews/)
- **Localization:** rewriting all of the above for each market. [Localization guide](/store-page/localization/)
- **Testing:** Product Page Optimization and Google Play store listing experiments. [Testing guide](/store-page/testing/)

## How the two stores differ

| Topic | App Store | Google Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search reads | Name, subtitle, keyword field, category, plus user behavior | Name, short and full description, plus user behavior |
| Hidden keyword field | Yes, 100 bytes | No |
| One-line summary | Subtitle, 30 characters | Short description, 80 characters |
| Store page tests | Product Page Optimization: icon, screenshots, previews | Store listing experiments: graphics and, in localized tests, descriptions |
| Audience pages | Up to 70 custom product pages | Up to 50 custom store listings |

## The order to work in

1. Write one sentence on what the app does and who it's for.
2. Collect the words customers use, from reviews, support and store search suggestions.
3. Write the subtitle or short description, then the name and keywords.
4. Make the first three screenshots show the outcome, the main screen and the difference.
5. Check every claim against the shipped build.
6. Launch, record a baseline, then test one change at a time.
7. Judge changes by retention as well as conversion.

## What not to believe

- No tool has Apple's or Google's real search volume. Popularity scores are estimates.
- A ranking change rarely has a single cause you can prove.
- A higher conversion rate is a loss if the new installs leave in a day.
- Repeating a keyword in your Google Play description doesn't help, and can break the metadata policy.

Start with the [launch checklist](/launch/checklist/) if you're shipping, or the [store page guides](/store-page/) if you're improving a live app.
