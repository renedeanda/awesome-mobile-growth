# Google Play listing

Verify live limits in Play Console Help before publishing. Common limits are app name 30 characters, short description 80, and full description 4,000.

Review the listing as a system: name and short description establish relevance; graphics and screenshots establish comprehension and confidence; the full description supports evaluation. Do not stuff repeated or irrelevant terms. Do not infer search demand from autocomplete alone.

For creative assets, distinguish the minimum needed to publish from requirements that affect promotional eligibility. Record device type, orientation, language, and the product evidence visible in every frame.

Primary sources: Google Play Console Help, “Create and set up your app” and “Add preview assets to showcase your app.”
