# Evidence and claims

Use these labels:

- **Official** — stated in current first-party platform documentation.
- **Observed** — directly seen in a named interface, store surface, or dataset at a recorded date.
- **Measured** — produced by a defined experiment or analysis with its scope and caveats.
- **Hypothesis** — testable explanation, not yet established.
- **Recommendation** — judgment based on the available evidence.

For volatile facts, record source, access date, affected platform or locale, and uncertainty. Never convert correlation into causation or a tool estimate into a platform fact.
