# Experiments

Write the decision before the variant.

An experiment brief contains: decision, observation, hypothesis, one controlled change, audience and locale, primary measure, quality guardrail, observation window or stopping rule, implementation notes, result, and next move.

Avoid testing several unrelated ideas in one variant. Do not call a directional result universal truth. Note traffic limits, seasonality, acquisition mix, novelty, and platform-specific methodology. Preserve losing results; they are part of the evidence.
