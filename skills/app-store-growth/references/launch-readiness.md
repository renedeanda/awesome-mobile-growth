# Launch readiness

Review in this order:

1. Product facts and supported devices.
2. Store record, identifiers, version, territories, pricing, privacy, and policy declarations.
3. Metadata limits, accuracy, localization, and links.
4. Screenshots, video, device fit, and real-build evidence.
5. Test path, review notes, demo account, purchases, and platform-specific entitlements.
6. Release method, monitoring owner, rollback or hotfix path, and post-launch learning date.

Separate blockers from recommendations. Link every rule-based blocker to current primary documentation.
